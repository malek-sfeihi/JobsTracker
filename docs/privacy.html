JobsTracker – Privacy policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

JobsTracker is a free, non-commercial application that helps job seekers follow their applications. Each user connects their own Gmail account and sees only their own data.

What data the app accesses

With your explicit permission, through Google sign-in, JobsTracker reads Gmail messages using the read-only scope (gmail.readonly). It only looks at emails that may be related to job applications: sender, subject, date and text content. The app cannot send, delete or modify any email.

How the data is used

The emails are used only to detect job applications, group them by company and position, and display their status (waiting, rejected, interview…) in a private dashboard. The data is never used for advertising, never sold and never shared with third parties.

Where the data is stored

Data is stored in a private database hosted in the European Union (Neon PostgreSQL, Frankfurt). Each user's data is kept separate and is only shown to that user, after signing in with their Google account. Access credentials are kept secret and are never published.

Google API Services

JobsTracker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google account permissions. To have all stored data deleted, send a request to the contact address below.
